# Privacy and External Services

Zibll includes optional integrations that can contact external services only when configured and enabled by the site administrator.

Potential integrations include payment gateways, SMS providers, social login providers, map/IP lookup providers, content-audit providers, Meilisearch, and logistics APIs.

Depending on configuration, data sent to third-party services may include order/payment metadata, phone numbers for SMS, OAuth tokens or identifiers, IP addresses, submitted content for audit, search-index data, or shipment information.

Administrators should review the privacy terms of each configured provider and disclose enabled integrations in their site privacy policy.
